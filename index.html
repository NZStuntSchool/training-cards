New to Open Stunt Gym?

Open to all stunt-trained performers

NZ Stunt School Open Stunt Gym is an opportunity for stunt-trained performers to maintain and develop their skills, explore new ideas, practise with others and connect with the stunt community.

Whether you want to work on fight performance, falls, reactions, movement, acting or create your own mini action scenarios, there's plenty to explore.

These are simple training ideas. They do not replace guided training sessions by professionals.

You don't need to follow a structured class. Choose your own training ideas, work at your own level and make the most of the session. An instructor will be on site to assist and supervise where needed.

Open Stunt Gym rules

  • These are simple training ideas. They do not replace guided training sessions by professionals. Only work on what you've already been taught.
  • Large stunt equipment is available during the session (wires, deadman and mini high falls for trained performers only).
  • An instructor is on site to assist and supervise where needed. Train within your own ability and only skills you've been taught.
  • Wires, the deadman and mini high falls are only for people trained or assessed by NZSS on that equipment. "Pick for me" and "Give me 3 ideas" never choose them.
  • Cards marked "Bring a buddy or more" need at least one stunt-trained partner. More buddies is fine, just take turns. No buddy, no partner work. Pick a solo card instead.
  • Rubber or training weapons only. Treat every replica as real.
  • Put gear back where it lives. Report damage straight away.
  • Unsure about anything? Stop and ask the instructor before you go again.
